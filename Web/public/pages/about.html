This page will explain the project: its aims, the books it covers, and how the data is made.

The data

Each book is split into dated diary entries. Pipelines then pull out place names, people, organisations, stays and incidents. Place names are matched to real-world coordinates where possible, and the rest are kept as places without a location.

Caveats

All of this is generated automatically and is still being reviewed. Matches, dates and relationships can be wrong.
