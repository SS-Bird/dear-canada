Dear Canada

Canadian history through a children’s diary series

The Dear Canada books tell Canadian history through the fictional diaries of girls living through it. This site maps where those diaries go and when, and how the people, places and events in them connect.

Right now it is a working space for exploring and sharing the metadata extracted from the books. Expect rough edges and data that changes often.
